Facebook’s About line matches the locked times, with two differences that were not copied onto the site:

- It says “Wednesday Prayer Meeting 6:30p.” The church website and this site say Wednesday worship at 6:30 PM.
- It says “Yoke (Wednesday) 6-8p” and “Kid Connection.” The published YOKE window stays 6:30–8:00 PM. Kids Connection stays the name already on the site.

Facebook About also names Pastor Brother Aaron Terlecki and Youth Pastor Brother Mason Cook.
