On Mission

We support missionaries and send teams. The call isn’t only for “someone else” — Christ bought us at a price, and His love compels us to live for Him and point others to Him.

Missions We Support

At Burns Baptist Church, we believe sharing the Gospel extends far beyond the walls of our church. Through prayer, financial support, mission trips, and community outreach, we partner with missionaries and ministries who are sharing the love of Jesus Christ both locally and around the world.
