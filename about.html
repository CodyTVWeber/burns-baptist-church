About

Southern Baptist. About 200 people. Large families. Country — not fancy. We exist to love God, love people, and make disciples.

Our story

In 2015, Pastor Aaron “A.T.” Terlecki brought Hillcrest Baptist into Burns Baptist. Same gospel. One faith family on Hwy 96 in Burns, Tennessee — part of the JST Baptist Association.

We’re still that kind of church: parking lot, foyer boxes, kids in the halls, preaching that says you need Jesus.

On mission

We support missionaries and send teams. The call isn’t only for “someone else” — Christ bought us at a price, and His love compels us to live for Him and point others to Him.
