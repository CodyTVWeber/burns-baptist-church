Church Leadership

Pastor

Aaron “A.T.” Terlecki

Pastor A.T. has a heart for sharing the Gospel, serving others, and helping people grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ. He is passionate about teaching God’s Word and leading Burns Baptist Church in faith, fellowship, and service. Pastor A.T. and his wife, Lori, are deeply committed to the church family and to seeing lives changed by the truth of God’s Word.

Youth Pastor

Mason Cook

Mason serves as the Youth Pastor at Burns Baptist Church. He has a heart for students and is committed to helping the next generation know and follow Jesus Christ, build a strong Biblical foundation, and find their place in the local church. Mason is married to Bailey, and they enjoy being part of the Burns Baptist Church family. He has served on staff since April 2023.

Worship Minister

Dr. Grant Allen

Dr. Grant Allen serves as our Worship Minister, leading our church family in Christ-centered worship each week. He has a passion for using music to glorify God, encourage believers, and point others to the Gospel through worship. Grant and his wife, Candice, are blessed with five children, and they are actively involved in the life and ministry of Burns Baptist Church.

Our story

Burns Baptist Church is a Southern Baptist congregation in Burns, Tennessee, and part of the JST Baptist Association.

In 2015, Pastor Aaron “A.T.” Terlecki brought Hillcrest Baptist into Burns Baptist. Same gospel. One church family on Hwy 96.

We gather to worship, study God’s Word, and help one another follow Jesus. You are welcome here.

On mission

We support missionaries and send teams. The call isn’t only for “someone else” — Christ bought us at a price, and His love compels us to live for Him and point others to Him.
